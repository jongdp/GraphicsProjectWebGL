Controls for camera movement:
W / S: move forward / backward
A / D: move left / right
R / F: move up / down

Controls for camera rotation (Use the arrow keys):
Up / Down Arrow: look up / down (pitch)
Left / Right Arrow: turn left / right (yaw)
